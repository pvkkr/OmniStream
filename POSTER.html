Why I Created This

No More Shady Telegram Bots or Ad Websites

"I was completely tired of using sketchy websites filled with fake download buttons and random Telegram bot channels that spam you with ads, slow down, or stop working just to download a simple clip from Instagram, Twitter, or YouTube."

OmniStream replaces all of that with a clean, private desktop app that lives right on your laptop screen. Paste a link, pick your quality, and watch or download immediately.

Everything It Can Do for You

🔗

Works with Virtually Any Video Link

Paste links from YouTube, Instagram Reels, X (Twitter), TikTok, Reddit, Vimeo, Facebook, or any web video. It fetches the video preview, channel name, and duration in seconds.

📺

Full Quality Selection with File Sizes

Choose exactly how sharp you want your video: 4K Ultra HD, 2K, Full HD (1080p), HD (720p), or Standard (480p). It even calculates the exact download file size in advance.

🎵

Download Songs & Audio as Crystal-Clear MP3

Love the background music or podcast audio in a clip? Switch to the Audio tab and download it as high-quality MP3 (320 kbps) or original audio with one click.

🍿

Stream in VLC with Smooth Timeline Seeking

Watch any video directly inside your laptop's VLC Player without waiting to download. Jump backward and forward along the timeline freely without the video restarting from the beginning.

⚡

Convenient One-Click Desktop Launcher

Double-click the shortcut on your desktop, paste your copied link, and hit start. When downloading completes, tap "Play in VLC" or "Open Folder" instantly.

🛡️

Zero Ads, Zero Spam, 100% Private

Everything runs directly on your laptop. No accounts to create, no tracking, no subscription fees, no popups, and no sketchy bots stealing your data.

How to Use (As Simple as 1-2-3):
1 Copy any video link & paste it
2 Pick your quality (4K, 1080p, MP3)
3 Click Stream in VLC or Download
